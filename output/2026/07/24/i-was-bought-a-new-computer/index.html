I was bought a new computer

Twelve years ago I wrote about getting a new computer and now I have another new one. I didn't buy this one, though; it was a fiftieth birthday present from my parents. And I'm really rather pleased with it.

First, let's have a look. Of the three things I'm most pleased with, the first is the look of it, which is all down to the case.

Case FRACTAL TERRA Mini ITX Case (JADE)
Processor (CPU) AMD Ryzen 7 9700X Eight Core CPU (3.8GHz-5.5GHz/40MB CACHE/AM5)
Motherboard ASUS® ROG STRIX B850-I GAMING WIFI (AM5, DDR5, M.2 PCIe 5.0, Wi-Fi 7)
Memory (RAM) 64GB Corsair VENGEANCE RGB DDR5 6000MHz CL30 (2 x 32GB)

lots of memory. I am a big believer in having plenty of RAM. There are people who will tell you that you don't need it unless you're running many VMs, and I do not agree with these people. Can't have too much RAM; that was a thing I did right about the last machine, and I've done the same here.

Power Supply CORSAIR SF750 750W 80 PLUS® Platinum Certified High Performance SFX PSU
Monitor IIYAMA XUB2492HSU-B6 24" LED WIDESCREEN, DP, HDMI 1920x1080

a second monitor. So now I have two. Sweet.

Graphics Card 12GB ASUS PRIME GEFORCE RTX 5070 OC - HDMI, 3 x DP

This is the second good thing; a good graphics card. This has gee'd up my Blender experience enormously and is very pleasing to me, and also has allowed experimenting with local LLM stuff in order to try to form an informed opinion on this sort of thing.

1st M.2 SSD Drive 500GB CRUCIAL T500 GEN 4 M.2 NVMe PCIe SSD (up to 7200MB/sR, 5700MB/sW)
2nd M.2 SSD Drive 4TB CORSAIR CORE XT MP600 NVMe PCIe M.2 SSD (up to 5000 MB/R, 4400 MB/W)

And the third good thing is all the disc space. The thing I really screwed up with the last build was penny-pinching on hard drive space; I was forever deleting stuff in order to have room to download more. I wasn't going to make that mistake again. And indeed I have not; I haven't even used up 25% of the space yet, and I can blithely download whatever I want and not have to worry about tidying it up, emptying the rubbish bin, all that. It's so freeing.

As with last time, I went to PC Specialist, and they did a good job. Now my lovely machine sits on my desk and looks pretty and does everything I want. Cheers, mum and dad; here's to the next twelve years of it.
